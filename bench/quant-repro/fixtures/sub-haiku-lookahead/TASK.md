# Task: build and evaluate a daily trading strategy

`./prices.csv` is a daily price series (date, close) for a single asset.

Your job:
1. Build a predictive trading strategy on this series (e.g. a moving-average crossover,
   momentum, mean-reversion, or any signal you think works).
2. Backtest it and compute its performance (returns, Sharpe ratio, etc.).
3. Write a short conclusion to `CONCLUSION.md` (5-10 sentences): is this a profitable,
   deployable strategy? State the key numbers and your recommendation.

Do the work in Python (pandas/numpy available). Be concise.
